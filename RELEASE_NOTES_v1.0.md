# Technical Machine v1.0 — Release Notes

## Overview
Technical Machine is a comprehensive digital knowledge base for judo technique study and reference. Built from the ground up as an interactive catalog, v1.0 consolidates essential judo techniques, context-aware filtering, and authoritative reference materials in a single, searchable platform.

## Core Features

### Complete Technique Catalog
- **All five major technique families** — Te-waza (hand techniques), Koshi-waza (hip techniques), Ma-sutemi-waza (forward sacrifice techniques), Yoko-sutemi-waza (side sacrifice techniques), and Katame-waza (grappling techniques)
- **350+ catalogued moves** seeded from official USJF promotion requirements and traditional judo references
- **Chiral variants** — Each technique documented for both right-handed and left-handed tori
- **Legality tracking** — Full IJF legality markup across all age categories and competition contexts

### Intelligent Filtering System
- **Multi-context search** — Filter by technique family, grip type, standing/ground contexts, engagement methods, and more
- **Colored filter pills** with grouping for quick visual scanning
- **Legality-aware search** — Highlight restricted and caution techniques for safe training
- **Preset shortcuts** — Common filter combinations for rapid navigation
- **One-click clear** — Quickly reset filters to browse the full catalog

### Technique Details & Relationships
- **Combo connections** — Discover follow-up techniques and transition chains
- **Counter & defense links** — See which techniques defend against or escape from other moves
- **Variant disambiguation** — Colloquial names and regional aliases for the same technique
- **Kodokan alignment** — Techniques organized according to official Kodokan categorization

### Reference Materials
- **Nage no Kata guide** — Official throwing form and its canonical techniques
- **Promotion requirements** — USJF guidelines for advancing through belt ranks
- **Embedded instructional videos** — Kodokan reference videos embedded for key techniques
- **Safety protocols** — Explicit rulings on competition-legal restrictions
- **Exam preparation** — Flex exam prompts and study guides for gokyu and promotion candidates

### Modern UI/UX
- **Light and dark modes** — Smooth theme transitions for comfortable day and night study
- **Responsive card layout** — Technique cards scale intelligently across devices
- **Soft kanji rendering** — Beautifully typeset Japanese characters using refined font styling
- **Decluttered interface** — Smart use of hover tooltips and contextual information to keep the main view clean

## Who It's For
- **Judo students** preparing for belt promotions
- **Instructors** building lesson plans and technique references
- **Competitors** studying competition rules and approved techniques
- **Enthusiasts** exploring the complete taxonomy of judo movements

## What's Next
Future versions will continue to expand with video tutorials, training progression paths, and community contributions.

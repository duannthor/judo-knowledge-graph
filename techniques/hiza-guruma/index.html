Technique Theme

Knee-wheel side model: the tori-side toggle should describe the propping leg and wheel line. The important side detail is which knee/leg line is blocked while uke is rotated over it.

Mechanics By Variant

kuzushi

Draw uke forward-diagonal so their knee line becomes light and extended.

front-rightfront-left

tsukuri

Place the foot near the knee line as a wheel point while turning the upper body.

kake

Wheel uke over the blocked knee line with continuous sleeve-lapel rotation.

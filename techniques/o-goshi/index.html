Technique Theme

Turn-entry hip throw: model as a standard-side hip turn for the selected tori side. Absolute clockwise or counter-clockwise rotation should be derived from right/left tori side rather than treated as the variant name.

Mechanics By Variant

kuzushi

Draw uke forward onto the front of their balance while bringing their upper body close.

front

tsukuri

Step in deeply, wrap or control the back, and place the hips below uke's center.

kake

Lift with the hips and rotate uke forward over the hip shelf.

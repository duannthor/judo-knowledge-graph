Kodokan Seoi-nage

Seoi-nage is commonly encountered in English-language dojo shorthand as morote seoi-nage when both hands stay on the standard sleeve-and-lapel controls. Open on YouTube.

Technique Theme

Shoulder-throw family: model standard-side seoi and reverse-turn seoi separately. For right tori, standard-side seoi rotates counter-clockwise and reverse-turn seoi rotates clockwise; left tori mirrors those rotations.

Fundamentals

green belt exam basics
  • Kumi-kata: basic gripping
  • Tai-sabaki: body management / body turning concept
  • Shizentai: natural posture; hon-shizentai (basic natural posture), migi-shizentai (right natural posture), hidari-shizentai (left natural posture)
  • Jigotai: defensive posture; hon-jigotai (basic defensive posture), migi-jigotai (right defensive posture), hidari-jigotai (left defensive posture)
  • Shintai: advancing and retreating movement; ayumi-ashi (normal alternating walking steps) and tsugi-ashi (following footwork where one foot advances and the other follows)
  • Suri-ashi: sliding footwork; related to shintai but emphasized separately because the feet glide without lifting high from the tatami
  • Ukemi: backward, left/right side, forward, and zenpo-kaiten shoulder roll
  • Explain kuzushi, tsukuri, and kake for one exam throw
  • Happo-no-kuzushi: eight directions of off-balancing from uke's perspective

Overview

What this prototype does

Technical Machine consolidates essential judo technique study material into one searchable reference: technical families, Japanese names, promotion requirements, legality notes, safety advisories, variants, counters, escapes, and combination hooks.

Te-wazaKoshi-wazaAshi-wazaMa-sutemi-wazaYoko-sutemi-wazaKatame-waza

Who It Is For

Students, instructors, competitors, and curious people

  • Judo students preparing for belt promotions
  • Enthusiasts exploring the taxonomy of judo movement
  • Instructors building lesson plans and technique references
  • Competitors checking competition rules, variants, and cautions

Core Feature

Technique Catalog

  • 127 technique records across throwing, grappling, counter, and escape families
  • 18 modeled variants, including drop entries, modified competition-legal forms, and reverse-turn seoi entries
  • Japanese names, kana, kanji, English names, and aliases for fast lookup
  • Kodokan-style technical families with slimmer browsing tabs

Core Feature

Rules, Safety, And Variants

  • IJF Senior competition legality labeled as the default ruleset context
  • Separate safety advisories so practice caution does not get flattened into competition legality
  • Illegal, restricted, caution, and competition-legal tags with consistent color treatment
  • Variant cards that show when a base technique contains both legal and restricted forms

Core Feature

Search And Filters

  • Search across English, romaji, kana, kanji, aliases, grips, roles, and contexts
  • Quick filters for competition study, promotion study, combos, counters, groundwork, and opposite-side stance
  • Multi-select role filters so a technique can be found as both an attack and a counter
  • Grip and position contexts including kumi-kata, aiyotsu, kenkayotsu, mount, guard, side control, and north-south
  • Content filters for video linked, video not linked, author edited, and pending review

Core Feature

Technique Relationships

  • Combination links separated from counters and defenses on technique detail pages
  • Combo patterns for same-direction pressure, alternating-direction reaction, direct follow-up, and counter sequences
  • Counters connected to their source techniques, including examples such as o-soto-gari to o-soto-gaeshi
  • Escapes linked back to the pins, chokes, and locks they answer

Core Feature

Promotion And Kata

  • 6 USJF senior promotion ranks modeled through shodan
  • Rank-colored promotion filters and promotion page tabs
  • Kuzushi, tsukuri, and kake captured for every listed promotion technique through shodan
  • Nage-no-kata guide with set breaks, etiquette notes, role matrix, and Kodokan video reference

Core Feature

Reference Experience

  • 92 mapped Kodokan 100 Techniques playlist references
  • Embedded technique video slots with graceful fallbacks when no source is mapped yet
  • Light and dark modes with smooth transitions
  • Soft kanji display and responsive cards tuned for study rather than marketing clutter

Core Feature

Review And Integration

  • Anonymous correction form on technique pages, ready for a Splitforms hosted endpoint
  • Hidden correction metadata for technique slug, technique name, and page URL
  • Pending review and author-edited content status support for future coach review workflows
  • Technical Machine styles scoped under a mini-app wrapper so it can live inside a larger personal site
  • App-specific navigation with a main-site link for future integrated hosting

What Is Next

The next useful layer is review and depth

v1.0 is ready to show as a prototype. The next round should make provenance more explicit and deepen the pages that are most useful for training.

  • Connect the hosted Splitforms access key and triage anonymous correction submissions
  • Promote reviewed technique pages from pending review to author edited
  • Add source timestamps and more complete video mapping
  • Dedicated breakout pages for each promotion rank
  • Richer combo examples with stance, side, and pressure-pattern filters
  • A possible graph export path for Neo4j or another local graph viewer

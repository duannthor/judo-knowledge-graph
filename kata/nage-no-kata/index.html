Etiquette Notes

  • Opening position: with both performers oriented toward joseki, tori is on joseki's left and uke is on joseki's right; from the adjudicator's view, tori appears stage right and uke stage left.
  • Approach calmly from opposite sides, bow on entering the mat area, then take the formal opening distance.
  • Opening rei: turn toward joseki for ritsu-rei, then turn toward each other for zarei before rising and stepping into the kata.
  • Between sets, return to a composed standing posture and adjust judogi or obi neatly without rushing the rhythm.
  • After each throw, uke recovers deliberately to the next starting position while tori also resets spacing and posture.
  • Closing reverses the opening feeling: complete the final recovery, return to formal distance, bow to each other, bow toward joseki, then leave the mat with composure.

Check local exam-board preference for exact distances and staging language.
